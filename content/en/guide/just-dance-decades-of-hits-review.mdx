## Adventure Mode, Extreme Versions, and How the Modes Stack Up

The biggest structural change is Adventure mode. According to the reveal posts, you begin the journey by dancing to "Supersonic" by Lyra Gigavolt, then travel across the decades. Later, a legendary duo joins for "Across All Time." That framing implies progression: you unlock eras as you go rather than scrolling a flat menu of songs.

| Mode or feature | What it appears to do | Best suited for |
|---|---|---|
| Adventure mode | Story-driven progression across eras | Solo players who want structure |
| Standard routines | Classic single-song play | Quick sessions and warm-ups |
| Extreme Versions | Higher-intensity choreography | Veterans chasing a challenge |
| Alternate Versions | Reworked staging of a known track | Players who want variety |
| Duet routines | Two-dancer choreography | Pairs and co-op sessions |

The naming convention is worth noting. "Take Away Version" and "Extreme Version" both imply that the same song can exist in multiple choreographies with different pacing, camera work, and stamina demands. If you have played earlier entries, this is familiar territory — but the fact that both variants appeared in the marketing suggests the roster will lean heavily on them.

## Tips to Score Higher in Just Dance: Decades of Hits

Scoring well is less about athleticism than about rhythm consistency and knowing how the game reads your movement. These habits apply across every mode.

| Tip | Why it works | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Watch the pictograms, not the dancer | The on-screen coach is a distraction; the pictogram is the actual instruction | Low |
| Warm up with two standard routines | Cold shoulders cost you accuracy in the first 30 seconds | Low |
| Save Extreme Versions for last | Fatigue compounds; accuracy drops sharply when you are tired | Medium |
| Learn the chorus before the verse | Choruses repeat and carry the most scoring weight | Medium |
| Play duets with a partner early | Timing cues are easier to read with a second body on screen | Medium |
| Replay one song three times | Muscle memory beats song variety for score gains | Medium |

A few extra pointers that experienced players swear by:

- Keep your playing space clear and consistent — changing rooms changes your camera calibration and your scores.
- Use the same controller or tracking method across sessions so your movement profile stays stable.
- In Adventure mode, do not rush the early eras. The tutorials embedded in the opening routines teach the scoring window.
- If a song feels impossible, drop to the standard version first, then return to the Extreme Version once the chorus timing is burned in.
