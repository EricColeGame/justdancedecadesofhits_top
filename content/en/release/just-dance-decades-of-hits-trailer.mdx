## How Decades of Hits Compares to the Yearly Just Dance Games

The annual Just Dance model is predictable by design: a new numbered or dated entry each cycle, a setlist weighted toward the past twelve months of chart success, and a fresh interface. Decades of Hits changes the pitch rather than the mechanics.

| Feature | Yearly Just Dance edition | Just Dance: Decades of Hits |
|---|---|---|
| Setlist focus | One year of chart hits | Multiple eras of dance music |
| Main appeal | Staying current | Nostalgia plus variety |
| Naming style | Numbered or dated | Themed |
| Party fit | Great for current-music crowds | Great for mixed-age rooms |

That last row is the interesting one. A yearly edition is easy to recommend because the buyer knows exactly what they are getting. A themed release has to earn its place by curating well. If the tracklist is sharp, Decades of Hits could become the copy people keep on the shelf for a decade instead of replacing every year.

There is also a practical upside for households. A multi-era setlist means a teenager, a parent, and a grandparent can all find a song they recognize in the same session — which is exactly the scenario party games are built for.
