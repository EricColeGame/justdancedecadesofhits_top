## Why Just Dance Decades of Hits Nintendo Switch 2 Stands Out

Plenty of dance games lean on a single era or a single genre. Just Dance: Decades of Hits takes the opposite approach, treating each decade as its own themed world with a distinct visual identity and a hand-picked tracklist. You can hop between musical eras or build one long playlist that runs from 1970s disco-pop to current chart hits.

The result is a game that plays well at multigenerational gatherings. A grandparent, a teenager, and a nine-year-old can all find something they recognize on the same menu screen — which is exactly the pitch the developers are making with the "all generations" framing.

| Feature | What It Actually Means for Players |
|---|---|
| Six decades of music | Each era gets its own look and curated song list, so the game feels like several themed collections in one |
| Adventure Mode | A story-driven journey through six themed worlds with mini-activities and unlockable rewards |
| Local play for up to 6 | One console, one TV, a whole room of dancers |
| Party Mode and Workout Mode | Two shortcut structures for casual hangouts and exercise sessions |
| Switch 2 enhancements | Higher resolution, smoother frame rates, and faster menu navigation |

For a series that has always been about accessibility, the decade structure solves a real problem: new players freeze up when they don't know a song. Here, almost every track is one somebody in the room has heard before.

## What the Limited Edition (Target Exclusive) Includes

The Limited Edition is a retailer-exclusive version of the game, and its headline bonus is the 2000s Digital Cosmetic Pack. That pack lets you dress up your Dancer Card and scoring effects with items inspired by early-2000s pop culture — think the aesthetic of frosted tips, flip phones, and boy-band choreography rather than gameplay advantages.

It's worth being precise about what this edition is and isn't. The cosmetics are purely visual. They don't unlock songs, shortcuts, or extra modes, and they don't change scoring. Ubisoft also notes that the bonus content may be offered separately or as a giveaway at its own discretion, and that the offer is subject to change — so treat the pack as a nice extra rather than the reason to buy.

If you're buying Just Dance Decades of Hits Nintendo Switch 2 primarily for the cosmetics, the Limited Edition is the only way to get that specific pack bundled in. If you just want the songs and modes, the extras won't change your experience much.

### Adventure Mode: A Story-Driven Tour Through the Decades

Adventure Mode is the biggest structural addition in Just Dance: Decades of Hits, and it's exclusive to this entry. Instead of picking songs from a menu, you dance your way through a cheerful, story-driven journey across six themed worlds.

Each world mixes standard dance routines with mini-activities and unlockable rewards, giving solo players a reason to keep coming back beyond chasing high scores. If you've ever wanted a campaign structure in a dance game, this is the closest the series has come. It's also a smart on-ramp for younger players who respond better to progression than to leaderboards.

### What You Need Before You Play

This is the part that trips people up, so read it before you buy. The retail package is a download code, not a game card. You'll need the following in place to redeem and play.

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Internet connection | Needed to redeem the code and for ongoing play |
| Ubisoft account | Required to access the game and online features |
| Nintendo account | Required to redeem the download code |
| Platform subscription | Required for online multiplayer and online features (sold separately) |
| Persistent internet | Required, per the publisher's disclaimers |

The persistent-connection requirement is the biggest practical caveat for families with unreliable Wi-Fi or for anyone hoping to play offline at a cabin. Plan accordingly.

### Quick Setup Checklist

- Create or sign in to your Ubisoft account before you open the box.

## FAQ: Just Dance Decades of Hits Nintendo Switch 2

**Do I need a Nintendo Switch 2 to play Just Dance: Decades of Hits?**

The Switch 2 release is specifically enhanced for that console, with higher resolution, smoother frame rates, and faster navigation. If you're shopping for the version with those performance upgrades, look for the Nintendo Switch 2 listing rather than assuming compatibility with older hardware.

**Does the Limited Edition come with a physical game card?**

No. The Limited Edition is a download-code product — the box does not contain a game card. You'll need an internet connection, a Ubisoft account, and a Nintendo account to redeem the code before you can play.

**How many people can dance at once?**

Up to six players can dance locally on one console. Party Mode is the easiest way to keep a group moving without stopping to pick songs, while Workout Mode suits smaller, more focused sessions.

**Is an internet connection required to play Just Dance Decades of Hits Nintendo Switch 2?**

Yes. The publisher lists a persistent internet connection and a Ubisoft account as requirements, and online multiplayer or online features also need an applicable platform subscription, sold separately. Offline play is not the intended setup here, so factor that into your purchase if your connection is inconsistent.
