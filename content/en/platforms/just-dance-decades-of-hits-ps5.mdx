## How to Dance on PS5 Without a Camera

This is the question that trips up returning players, especially anyone who last played on a PlayStation 3 or a Wii. There's no camera accessory to buy and no sensor bar to mount. Your phone is the controller.

| Step | What to Do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Buy and download Just Dance: Decades of Hits from the PlayStation Store |
| 2 | Install the free Just Dance Controller app on your smartphone |
| 3 | Put your phone and your PS5 on the same Wi-Fi network |
| 4 | Launch the game and follow the on-screen pairing prompt |
| 5 | Hold the phone in your right hand and mirror the coach on screen |
| 6 | Repeat the app install for each additional player (up to six) |

### Setup Tips That Actually Matter

- **Use the 5 GHz band if you can.** Six phones on one congested 2.4 GHz network is the most common cause of dropped inputs and mid-song disconnects.
- **Charge your phones first.** A two-hour dance session drains a battery faster than most people expect.
- **Give each player arm's length.** The app tracks movement, so cramped living rooms produce cramped scores.
- **Recalibrate if scores feel wrong.** Restarting the app fixes most pairing glitches faster than troubleshooting in-game.
- **Check for a day-one patch.** New releases almost always ship with a stability update within the first week.

## FAQ

**Is Just Dance: Decades of Hits available on PS5?**

Yes. Just Dance Decades of Hits PS5 is a current-generation release from Ubisoft, sold digitally through the PlayStation Store. Confirm your regional price and edition contents on the store page, since bundles can differ slightly between platforms.

**Do I need a PlayStation Camera or PS Move controllers?**

No. The game uses the free Just Dance Controller smartphone app instead of a camera peripheral. You need one phone per player, all connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your PS5.

**How many people can play at once?**

Up to six players locally in the same room, which is the same limit the series has used for several entries. Party Mode is built around that group size.

**Do I need a Just Dance+ subscription to enjoy the game?**

No. The base game includes the full six-decade tracklist and Adventure Mode. Just Dance+ is an optional subscription that adds a rotating catalog of extra songs, and it's bundled into the Deluxe and Ultimate editions if you want it included up front.

The bottom line: if your household has a PS5, a few phones, and a willingness to look ridiculous for twenty minutes, Just Dance: Decades of Hits is one of the easiest party purchases of the year — and the first one in a while that gives every generation at the party a song they already know.
