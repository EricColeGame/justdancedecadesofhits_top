## Decade-by-Decade Breakdown: What to Expect

Understanding the musical and choreographic style of each era will help you dominate the dance floor. Here’s a closer look at what each decade brings to the game.

### The 1970s: Disco Fever

The 70s set the foundation with groovy basslines and infectious disco beats. ABBA’s “Mamma Mia” and Redbone’s “Come and Get Your Love” are perfect examples of feel-good classics that get everyone moving. The choreography likely emphasizes fluid arm movements, hip sways, and partner-style interactions. This decade is ideal for warming up and finding your rhythm.

### The 1980s: Hip-Hop and Pop

Run DMC’s “It’s Tricky” brings early hip-hop energy, while Kool & The Gang’s “Celebration” adds a party anthem vibe. Expect sharp, rhythmic steps and a lot of attitude. The 80s are all about bold moves and iconic poses, so don’t be afraid to exaggerate your motions.

### The 1990s: Girl Power and Electronic

The Spice Girls’ “Wannabe” embodies the girl-power movement, while Daft Punk’s “Around the World” introduces French house. The 90s section mixes catchy pop choreography with repetitive electronic grooves. This is where timing becomes crucial—the beats are steady but require precise hits.

### The 2000s: Boy Bands and Beyond

*NSYNC’s “Bye Bye Bye” is the quintessential boy-band hit, complete with synchronized moves and dramatic hand gestures. The 2000s tracks often feature intricate group choreography that’s fun to replicate with friends. Expect plenty of nostalgia and pop hooks.

### The 2010s: EDM Explosion

Martin Solveig’s “Hello,” Pitbull’s “Give Me Everything,” and DJ Snake’s “Turn Down for What” define the EDM era. These songs are fast-paced and high-energy, with choreography that includes jumps, drops, and explosive movements. Stamina is key here—pace yourself.

### The 2020s: Viral Hits

Chappell Roan’s “HOT TO GO!” and Cult of Sound’s “Hi Lyfe” represent the modern, TikTok-driven era. The moves are trendy, often mimicking viral dance challenges. This section is perfect for younger players and anyone who loves contemporary pop culture.
