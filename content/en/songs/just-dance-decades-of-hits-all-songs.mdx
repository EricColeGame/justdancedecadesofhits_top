## Scoring Tips by Decade

Different eras punish different habits. Here's how to adjust.

- **'70s:** Choreography leans on sustained arm shapes and hip sway. Hold your poses to the end of the beat instead of snapping back early — the game rewards follow-through.
- **'80s:** Expect sharp, staccato isolations. Keep your movements small and crisp rather than large and loose.
- **'90s:** Group choreography means mirrored or alternating moves. Watch the coach's feet, not just their hands.
- **2000s:** Precision is everything. Syncopated hand patterns are where most players lose their streak.
- **2010s:** Pure endurance. Pace yourself in the first verse or you'll fade before the drop.
- **2020s:** Hybrid routines. Read the coach's silhouette — modern charts often switch styles mid-song.

If you only remember one thing: the era that feels easiest is usually the one whose movement style matches how you naturally dance, not the one with the slowest tempo.
