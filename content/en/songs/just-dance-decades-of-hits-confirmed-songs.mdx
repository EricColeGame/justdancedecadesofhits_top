## New Original Tracks and Cover Recordings

Not every track in a Just Dance game is a licensed original master. The series has a long history of using in-game cover recordings, credited to studio or fictional artist names. That practice continues here, and it is the single biggest source of confusion when fans compare lists.

| Type | What it means | How it appears in listings |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed original | The real artist's recording is used | Credited to the original artist |
| In-game cover | A re-recorded version made for the game | Credited to a studio name, sometimes "as" the original act |
| Original composition | A song written specifically for Just Dance | Credited to a fictional in-game performer |

Community reports also point to original compositions created for this entry, including tracks with names like "Across All Time" and "Across Time." Spelling, capitalization, and artist credits for these vary between sources — a classic sign that the information is circulating through player experience rather than an official reveal. Enjoy them, but do not treat the exact credits as final until you see them in-game.

## How to Tell a Real Confirmation From a Rumor

The phrase "confirmed" gets used very loosely in fan spaces. Here is a practical reliability ladder for anything you read about the tracklist.

| Source type | Reliability | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Official publisher page or trailer | Highest | Comes directly from the developer |
| Platform store listing | High | Legally vetted before publishing |
| Major gaming press | High | Usually sourced and fact-checked |
| Wiki tracklist pages | Medium | Often accurate, sometimes speculative |
| Fan playlists | Low | Frequently mix wish lists with real entries |
| Social media screenshots | Very low | Trivially edited, rarely sourced |

A few habits that will save you from disappointment:

- **Check the date.** A "confirmed" post from six months ago may have been walked back.
- **Look for the source link.** If there is none, downgrade it.
- **Watch for wish-list phrasing.** Words like "should," "needs," and "would be perfect" are predictions, not confirmations.
- **Cross-reference two independent sources.** One wiki and one playlist is not two sources if the playlist copied the wiki.

When you are tracking Just Dance Decades of Hits confirmed songs over a long reveal cycle, a simple spreadsheet with a source column beats scrolling through a dozen threads every night.
