## What Is Just Dance: Decades of Hits?

Just Dance: Decades of Hits is an era-spanning entry in Ubisoft's long-running dance franchise. Rather than building its tracklist around a single year or genre, it organizes everything by decade: 1970s disco and Motown, 1980s club and hip-hop, 1990s pop, 2000s boy bands and alternative, and 2010s EDM. The official tagline — "Every Era. One Floor" — captures the pitch almost perfectly.

That structure is exactly why the Just Dance Decades of Hits songs list feels different from a standard soundtrack. You aren't just collecting hits; you're moving through a timeline, and each decade brings its own tempo, attitude, and style of choreography.

For a group, that variety is the whole point. A 1970s track gets everyone singing along, an 80s rock anthem gets people jumping, and a 2010s drop track wipes the floor out right before the night ends.

## Tips for Nailing the Routines

Once you know the tracklist, the next step is actually scoring well. These habits make a measurable difference.

- **Watch the coach's hands, not their feet.** Most scoring windows are tied to arm and hand movement, so mirroring upper-body motion is more reliable than chasing footwork.
- **Learn the chorus first.** Nearly every routine repeats its chorus choreography, so mastering that section can carry an entire song.
- **Lock in the gold moves.** Each routine has specific highlighted moves worth bonus points — hitting those consistently is often the difference between a good score and a great one.
- **Play standing, not sitting.** The motion tracking is far more forgiving when you have full range of movement.
- **Replay one song rather than rotating randomly.** Three attempts on the same track beats one attempt on three different tracks.

If you're working through the Just Dance Decades of Hits songs list for the first time, pick one track per decade and drill it. You'll build a mental library of move patterns that transfer surprisingly well across routines.
