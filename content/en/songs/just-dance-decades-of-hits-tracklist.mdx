## What Is Just Dance: Decades of Hits?

Just Dance: Decades of Hits is a themed collection in the Just Dance family that organizes its song list around eras rather than around a single release year. Instead of jumping randomly between generations, the game groups tracks so one session can move from a 1970s disco floor to a 2010s festival stage without losing momentum.

Ubisoft's official page for the game leans into that idea with an "Every Era. One Floor" tagline, spotlighting one signature song per decade. The publisher also notes that the lineup pulls from a wide spread of styles — Latin, rock, hip-hop, and K-pop among them — so the decade framing never becomes a narrow nostalgia trip.

| Feature | What it means for players |
|---|---|
| Era-based organization | You can plan an entire night around one decade |
| Wide genre spread | Latin, rock, hip-hop, K-pop, and pop all appear |
| Mix of classics and modern hits | Something for every age in the room |
| Familiar play style | Same motion-based choreography as the rest of the series |

That structure is the reason the Just Dance Decades of Hits tracklist appeals to both longtime fans and people who have never held a controller. You do not need to know the discography — you just need to recognize a beat.
